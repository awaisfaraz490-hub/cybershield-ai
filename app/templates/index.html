FAQ

Can this tool tell me if my WhatsApp/Facebook has been hacked?

No. {{ app_name }} analyzes submitted content for phishing/scam indicators. It cannot check the security status of your actual accounts. If you suspect a compromise, change your password and contact the platform's official support.

Is my data safe?

We never ask for passwords, OTPs, or recovery codes. Scan history stores only a short, safe summary — not full messages or images.

How accurate is the risk score?

Scores are an AI/heuristic estimate based on known phishing patterns. They are not a scientific guarantee and should be used alongside your own judgment.
